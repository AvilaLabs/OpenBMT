# New customers

There is also a plain business reason to take bitcoin: some people want to
pay with it, and few shops let them.

## People who hold it and have few places to spend it

About one adult in ten in the United States used cryptocurrency in 2025,
to invest or to pay.[^shed] In surveys, 8% of UK adults held some in
2025,[^fca] and in the euro area 9.7% of households did in late
2024.[^ecb] These figures count every kind of cryptocurrency, not bitcoin
alone.

Very few of them spend it. In 2025, 2% of US adults used cryptocurrency to
pay for something or send money.[^shed] The reason they gave most often
was not speed, cost or privacy: it was that the person or business they
paid preferred it.[^shed] In 2024, more than a third of those who paid in
cryptocurrency gave that reason.[^kcfed] People pay in bitcoin where a
shop asks for it.

## Where they look

People who want to spend bitcoin look for places on maps made for that.
The largest, BTC Map, listed 26,914 places that take bitcoin worldwide in
August 2026.[^btcmap] That is a short list for a whole planet, and a shop
that takes bitcoin appears on it where those customers are looking.

Volunteers keep the map, and a listing is not a sale: of the places on
it, 16,243 had been checked by someone in the past year.[^btcmap]

## Visitors from abroad

A card from another country costs the shop more than a local one. Square
adds 1.5% for a card issued outside the United States, and Stripe adds
1.5% for an international card and another 1% to change the
currency.[^fees] Since the UK left the EU, card payments between the two
are no longer under the caps on what card issuers may charge.[^psr]

Bitcoin is the same money in every country. A visitor pays the price on
the screen from the wallet in their pocket, and the shop receives the
same bitcoin it would from a neighbour.

## What is not known

Nobody has measured well how many new customers taking bitcoin brings a
shop, and OpenBMT makes no promise about it. The best study of a whole
country is a warning against expecting much: in El Salvador in 2021 and
2022, where the law required every business to accept bitcoin, about 20%
did, and about 5% of their sales were paid in it.[^nber] For most shops,
bitcoin will be a small share of sales at first. What it costs the shop
to offer is set out in [What it asks of you](why-what-it-asks.md).
